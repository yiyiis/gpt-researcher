# Market Analysis

## Overview
Structured market analysis produces actionable insights, not just data dumps. Always ground conclusions in frameworks that force systematic thinking.

## When to Use
- Competitive landscape analysis
- Industry trend research
- Market sizing or segmentation
- Evaluating a company's market position
- Investment due diligence on a market

## Core Frameworks

### PEST Analysis (macro environment)
Assess four external forces:
- **P**olitical: regulations, trade policy, government stability
- **E**conomic: GDP, inflation, consumer spending, interest rates
- **S**ocial: demographics, lifestyle, cultural shifts
- **T**echnological: R&D, automation, disruption risk

### Porter's Five Forces (industry attractiveness)
1. Threat of new entrants (barriers to entry)
2. Bargaining power of suppliers
3. Bargaining power of buyers
4. Threat of substitutes
5. Competitive rivalry intensity

### SWOT (entity-level)
| Internal | External |
|----------|----------|
| **Strengths** | **Opportunities** |
| **Weaknesses** | **Threats** |

## Market Sizing Methods
- **TAM/SAM/SOM**: Total / Serviceable / Obtainable market
- **Bottom-up**: unit economics × potential customers
- **Top-down**: total market × target share

## Common Mistakes
- Confusing market size with revenue opportunity
- Ignoring substitute products outside the obvious category
- Extrapolating growth rates beyond reason
- Reporting data without interpretation
